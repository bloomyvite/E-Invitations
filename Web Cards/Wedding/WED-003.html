The First Hello

A rainstorm & one shared umbrella

A downpour, a crowded café awning, and James offering half his umbrella to a stranger with laughing eyes. The coffee went cold; the conversation never did.

Building Home

Two keys, one door

A little flat with crooked shelves, a rescue cat named Fig, and Sunday pancakes that somehow became a sacred tradition. Home stopped being a place — it became a person.

The Yes

Under the lantern festival

A thousand paper lanterns rising into the night, one knee on the cobblestones, one trembling question — and Elena’s yes, before he’d even finished asking.
